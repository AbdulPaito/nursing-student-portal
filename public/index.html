About Us - Marinduque State University

College of Allied Health Sciences (CAHS)

The College of Allied Health Sciences (CAHS) is a center of excellence committed to the development of competent, compassionate, and globally competitive Registered Nurses and Midwives. Grounded in strong values, faith, and service, CAHS proudly stands as "The Home of the Future Registered Professional Nurses and Midwives who are the Best for the Filipinos and the Choice of the World."

The College is dedicated to providing holistic education that integrates academic excellence, professional competence, ethical practice, and social responsibility. Through quality instruction, experiential learning, and values-based formation, CAHS prepares its students to respond effectively to the evolving demands of healthcare at the local, national, and international levels.

Vision, Mission, Goals, and Objectives (VMGO)

Guided by its Vision, Mission, Goals, and Objectives (VMGO), the College of Allied Health Sciences continuously strives to uphold quality education, relevant research, and meaningful community engagement in the field of health sciences.

Academic Programs Offered

The College of Allied Health Sciences offers academic programs designed to meet national and global healthcare standards:

  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN)
  • Bachelor of Science in Midwifery (BSM)

These programs aim to produce graduates who are skilled, ethical, and compassionate professionals capable of delivering quality healthcare services across diverse settings.

VMGO Wellness Park and Students' Study Center

In support of its commitment to student development and well-being, the VMGO Wellness Park and Students' Study Center was established as a space dedicated to learning, reflection, collaboration, and wellness.

This initiative was led by the CAHS and CAHSSO Administration and made possible through the Lord's provisions and the collective support of:

  • MSC Administration (GSO and PPMO)
  • CAHS Students Organization (CAHSSO)
  • MSC Male Nurses and Midwives Society
  • Supreme Student Council Federation
  • CAHS Alumni
  • Numerous supporters of CAHS

The Wellness Park and Study Center is dedicated to the present and future students of the College of Allied Health Sciences, serving as a nurturing environment that promotes academic focus, mental well-being, and community building.

Dedicated on 10 May 2024
Boac, Marinduque, Philippines

The PANACEAN Core Values

The College of Allied Health Sciences upholds the PANACEAN Core Values, which guide the character, conduct, and competence of its students, faculty, and staff:

P – Peace-loving and People-oriented
A – Agile and Altruistic
N – Nurturing and Noble-minded
A – Amiable and Available
C – Competent, Caring, and Compassionate
E – Excellent and Empathetic
A – Alert and Accountable
N – Neat and Non-judgmental

These values shape future health professionals who serve with integrity, empathy, professionalism, and excellence.
